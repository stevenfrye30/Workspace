Welcome to your mirror

This is a private record of your life — body, mind, health, relationships, money, and who you're becoming. It lives only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no one but you can see it.

Because it's yours alone, you keep it safe: every so often, open Forever-copy in the top bar and save the file somewhere permanent — a private drive, a folder you back up, wherever your lasting records live. That copy is what carries your record across browsers and across years.

A couple of parts are optional and off until you set them up — a read-only Google Calendar and a food-nutrition library. The README beside this page explains both. Everything else works right now.
